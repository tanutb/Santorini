      How to play

      Two players share a 5 × 5 island. Each controls two workers.

      1. Place. The first player places both workers on empty squares, then the second player does the same.
      2. Move. On your turn, move one worker to a neighbouring square, diagonals included. You may climb up one level or drop down any number. You cannot enter occupied squares or domes.
      3. Build. The worker you moved builds on a neighbouring empty square: one more level, or a dome on top of level three.
      4. Win. Move up onto level three. You also win if your opponent has no move they can complete.

      Teal rings show where you can go. Amber hexagons mark squares where your opponent could win next turn; dome them if you can. Drag to turn the island, or press Q and E. Ctrl + scroll zooms. F plays full screen.

      About the agent

      The agent is a neural network trained with our own PyTorch implementation of PPO-Clip, following OpenAI Spinning Up's algorithm: a policy network chooses moves and a separate value network judges positions. Illegal moves are masked out, so every choice it makes comes from the network's learned preferences, never from a scripted rule. Each game it sees the island from one of its eight rotations and mirror images, so its openings vary without changing how well it plays.

      How it fares

      Win rates over seat-balanced games against fixed opponents: random moves, a simple tactical player, and a one-move lookahead search.
